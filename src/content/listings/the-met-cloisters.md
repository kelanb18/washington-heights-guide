## What it is

The Met Cloisters is the Metropolitan Museum of Art's branch for medieval art, inside Fort Tryon Park. NYC Parks says it houses nearly 5,000 medieval works in a building made up of several structures from Europe. Wikipedia notes that Rockefeller reserved four acres in the center of the park for the museum, and that its collection includes the Unicorn tapestries.

## Who it's for

Visitors interested in medieval art, architecture, and gardens, and anyone pairing a museum visit with a walk through Fort Tryon Park. It is not on the Met's main Fifth Avenue campus, so plan to arrive by the M4 bus or a walk through the park.

## Standout details

The Met's visit information lists admission as pay-what-you-wish for New York State residents with a one-cent minimum (online purchase needs a New York billing address; bring proof of residency), and says the ticket includes same-day entry to both Met locations. It lists hours of 10am to 5pm Sunday through Tuesday and Thursday through Saturday, closed on Wednesday.

## Honest verdict

A memorable museum in a setting that is part of the pleasure, but it keeps limited hours and is closed on Wednesdays, Confirm hours, admission rules, and current exhibitions on the Met's site before going.
