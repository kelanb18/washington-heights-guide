## What it is

El Panadero Bakery is a neighborhood Dominican bakery at 1380 St Nicholas Ave, at the corner of W 179th St. The Infatuation's guide to the Little Dominican Republic calls it an Uptown gem.

## Who it's for

Anyone after a quick breakfast, an afternoon coffee, a slice of cake, or a sandwich to go on the 181st St side of the neighborhood.

## Standout details

The Infatuation says El Panadero is best known for tropical-flavored pastries, fluffy Dominican-style cakes like dulce de leche, and Cuban sandwiches, and suggests ordering a café con leche. City inspection records show an inspection on June 4, 2026.

## Honest verdict

A dependable bakery for pastries and a no-frills coffee. I found no official website or hours from a source I could open, so call (212) 923-6610 before going.
