## What it is

La Reina del Chicharrón is a counter-service Dominican restaurant at 1249 St Nicholas Ave. City inspection records list the business as Elsa Restaurant La Reina del Chicharron. The Infatuation says it has locations in Manhattan, New Jersey and the Bronx.

## Who it's for

Takeout customers and solo diners who want fried pork and a plate of sides, quickly and without ceremony.

## Standout details

The Infatuation's guide says the draw is the chicharrón, which it listed at $15 per pound in 2023, and suggests adding whatever looks good behind the counter, such as fried plantains, rice and mofongo. City inspection records show an inspection on May 4, 2026.

## Honest verdict

A very straightforward place, best for takeout. Prices and hours could have changed since the guide, and I could not find an official website, so call (212) 795-3667.
