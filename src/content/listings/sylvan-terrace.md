## What it is

Sylvan Terrace is a short cobbled street that runs from St. Nicholas Ave to Jumel Terrace, on the line of the old Morris estate carriage drive, where W 161st St would otherwise be. Wikipedia's entry on the Jumel Terrace Historic District says twenty wooden houses designed by Gilbert R. Robinson Jr. were built there in 1882–83, first rented to laborers and civil servants, and restored in 1979–81. They are now among the few frame houses left in Manhattan.

## Who it's for

Walkers, architecture fans, photographers, and anyone combining a visit to the Morris-Jumel Mansion with a few quiet minutes outside.

## Standout details

The district is a New York City landmark (1970) and is on the National Register of Historic Places (1973). The mansion is at the top of the lane.

## Honest verdict

A pretty, short stop that works best paired with the mansion. These are people's homes, so keep voices down and stay off the stoops.
