## What it is

La Dinastia is a Chino-Latino restaurant at 4059 Broadway that mixes Latin and Chinese-American dishes. The Infatuation calls it a neighborhood classic, and says the menu is written in both Spanish and English.

## Who it's for

Casual dinner groups and cheap-eats seekers who like rice-and-protein platters from both traditions.

## Standout details

The Infatuation's review describes fried pork chops with sweet plantains, tenderloin steak, breaded shrimp and General Tso's chicken, and highlights the special fried rice, made with tiny cubes of ham, baby shrimp and scallions. City inspection records show an inspection on May 4, 2026.

## Honest verdict

An old-school local favorite worth ordering from if you want something between Dominican and Cantonese-American cooking. I did not find an official website, so check hours by phone at (212) 928-6605.
