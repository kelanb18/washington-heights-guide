## What it is

Studio in the Heights is a fitness studio at 617 W 181st St. Its website says it was founded by Lisa and Roderick "Priest" Priestly and that it moved into this larger facility after about a decade in a small studio. It offers small-group classes (boxing, yoga, HIIT and strength), personal training and nutrition coaching.

## Who it's for

Neighbors who want a neighborhood gym with a coach's eye on form, people who prefer small classes to big-box gyms, and anyone mixing strength and boxing work.

## Standout details

The 2026 rates page lists a $45 non-member drop-in for its new Strength & Conditioning class. The class schedule runs early morning to evening on weekdays, a shorter Saturday morning schedule, and limited Sunday classes.

## Honest verdict

A local, founder-run alternative to a chain gym, though it is not cheap for drop-ins. The phone number on the site has an unusual area code, so book and confirm schedules through the website.
