## What it is

The St. Frances Cabrini Shrine is at 701 Fort Washington Ave. Its website says it is built on land Mother Cabrini purchased herself in 1899 and describes it as the only U.S. shrine where visitors can venerate her major relics. It has a chapel, a museum and exhibits on her mission, and a gift shop.

## Who it's for

Catholic pilgrims, school and parish groups, and anyone who wants a quiet place with Hudson views and a story about an Italian immigrant who built hospitals, schools and orphanages.

## Standout details

The shrine's website lists hours of Tuesday to Friday 10am to 5pm and Saturday and Sunday 9am to 5pm, closed Monday and major holidays, with the gift shop open Tuesday to Sunday 10am to 5pm. Mother Cabrini's feast is November 13, and the site notes a separate Mass schedule for celebrations on November 14 and 15.

## Honest verdict

A moving, quiet landmark that rewards a short detour, and one with a clear religious purpose. Check the Mass schedule on the website before a feast-week visit.
