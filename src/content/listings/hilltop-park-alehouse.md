## What it is

Hilltop Park Alehouse is a pub at 3821 Broadway, at the corner of W 159th St. It is named for the 1903 ballpark, Hilltop Park, that stood a few blocks away and was the original home of the team that became the New York Yankees, according to the city tourism board's listing. The Infatuation says it only opened in 2018 but looks as though it has been there forever, with dark wood and a penny-tile floor.

## Who it's for

Burger and beer people, brunch crowds, and baseball fans. The Infatuation's guide tagged it for birthdays, brunch, and date nights, and says it has a long list of beers and ciders and a wide menu focused on burgers, including a short-rib-and-brisket-blend burger, a salmon burger, and a beet veggie burger.

## Standout details

NYC's tourism listing says Tuesday is trivia night and there is live jazz on Sunday, and The Infatuation says there is brunch and live jazz on Sundays, with the buttermilk pancakes recommended. NYC's tourism listing also describes the bar as woman-owned and Latino-owned.

## Honest verdict

An easy, friendly place for a pint and a burger, and a good link to the neighborhood's baseball past. The hours come from aggregator listings and differ slightly between them (weekdays from noon to 11pm or midnight, weekends from 11am to midnight). City inspection records show an inspection in September 2025, and review excerpts dated early 2026 suggest it is still operating.
