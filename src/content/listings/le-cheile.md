## What it is

Le Chéile is an Irish pub at 839 W 181st St, on the western end of Hudson Heights' main commercial strip. Its website says it has served Washington Heights for over a decade and describes the name as meaning "together." A 2023 Infatuation guide described two floors, faded pink walls covered in old photos and posters, and a view of the George Washington Bridge.

## Who it's for

Neighbors looking for a casual pub, friends who want a place to stay a while, and people ending an evening near the 181st St A station. It is open until 2am Thursday through Sunday.

## Standout details

The Infatuation write-up listed shepherd's pie, bangers and mash, and burgers on the dinner menu and singled out the wings. The pub keeps an events calendar on its website. Confirm current dishes and happy-hour details with the pub.

## Honest verdict

Go for the room, the drinks, and a pub-style meal rather than for a destination menu: the same 2023 review said it mostly came back for the wings. Noise levels, crowds, and prices are not covered here, so check the pub's calendar for event nights.
