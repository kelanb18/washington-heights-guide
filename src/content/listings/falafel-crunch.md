## What it is

Falafel Crunch is a kosher Israeli restaurant at 2547 Amsterdam Ave, next to Yeshiva University's Wilf Campus. Its website says it operates under the supervision of the Va'ad Harabonim of Riverdale. The Jewish Link's December 2024 profile says it opened in January 2024 and is co-owned by David Nahon and Doron Levy, and The Commentator, YU's student paper, reported that it replaced Bagel Bar NYC at the address.

## Who it's for

YU students and staff, kosher-keeping families, and anyone who wants falafel, shawarma or a kebab sandwich near the 181st St and Amsterdam Ave stretch.

## Standout details

The Jewish Link highlights falafel, shawarma, kebab sandwiches, rice bowls, spicy meat soup, kibbeh and hummus, and says the restaurant accepts Yeshiva University cafeteria cards. A kosher directory lists it as a pareve establishment. Its website lists hours of Sunday to Thursday 11am to 11:30pm and Friday 11am to 4pm, and closed Saturday.

## Honest verdict

A solid, student-friendly kosher option with clear supervision and long hours. Hours can move around Shabbat and Jewish holidays, so check the website or call (212) 641-0155 first.
