## What it is

Dutch Baby Bakery, at 813 W 187th St, describes itself as a full-service restaurant, bar, and bakery serving seasonal Southern cooking. The website says everything is made in-house, from sandwich bread and pickles to biscuits and pulled pork, with brunch, dinner, bar, and bakery menus.

## Who it's for

People who want a bakery counter in the morning and a table later, and anyone ordering a custom cake: the site offers custom cakes, cupcakes, and cookies with a week's notice. It is on the same block as The Fountain Bookshop and hosts some of the bookshop's events.

## Standout details

The bakery's own copy points to buttermilk biscuits in the morning and craft cocktails at the end of the day. One visitor comment on the site recommends the egg-and-cheese biscuits and shortbreads; treat that as one reader's opinion. The site also posts a monthly bread calendar.

## Honest verdict

A useful all-purpose spot on the Hudson Heights uptown strip. One detail needs a check before you go: the site lists closing times of both 9pm and 10pm for Thursday through Sunday. Prices and the current menu are not verified here.
