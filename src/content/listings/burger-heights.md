## What it is

Burger Heights is a counter-service burger spot at 177 Wadsworth Ave, at W 182nd St. Its website says it was started by Ramon Pallone, who is from Naples, and that his landlord, Michael Vinocur, became a partner. The beef, turkey and lamb come from Pat LaFrieda, and the burgers are named after Upper Manhattan avenues. Gelato shakes are on the menu.

## Who it's for

People picking up a casual dinner, solo diners, and anyone who wants a burger outdoors on a nice evening.

## Standout details

The Infatuation's 2023 Washington Heights guide calls it a counter-service spot with thick beef, turkey and veggie patties topped with things like pineapple or guacamole, in a converted garage that is mostly takeout, with sidewalk seats along Wadsworth Ave. The website lists daily hours of 11:30am to 10pm.

## Honest verdict

A dependable, no-fuss burger place with a clear point of view and a nice sidewalk. Seating is limited, so expect to take it away or eat outside.
