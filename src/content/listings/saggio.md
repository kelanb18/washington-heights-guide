## What it is

Saggio is an Italian restaurant at 829 W 181st St, between Fort Washington Ave and Cabrini Blvd. The Infatuation describes a small, dark, candlelit dining room and a covered backyard.

## Who it's for

Neighbors looking for a low-key date, a group dinner with wine, or a table in the backyard in decent weather.

The Infatuation says big glasses of Italian wine and large portions of dishes like crostini and handmade pastas make it pleasant to hang out in, though it calls the food "generally unmemorable." City inspection records show an inspection on August 5, 2026, so it is operating.

A comfortable neighborhood Italian place rather than a food destination. The review dates from 2023 and I found no official website, so check hours and menu by phone at (212) 795-3080.
