## What it is

Carrot Top Pastries is a bakery at 3931 Broadway, on Broadway between W 164th and W 165th Sts, according to Patch. The Infatuation's 2023 guide says it has been open since 1979 and makes some of the best carrot cake the critic had had, with slices taller than a coffee mug, and that it also serves diner classics at breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

## Who it's for

Neighbors, medical center staff and visitors, and anyone who wants a slice of carrot cake. Columbia University Irving Medical Center's list of nearby local food options includes Carrot Top Pastries at 3931 Broadway with hours of 6am to 8pm.

## Standout details

Robert Mancino told the Manhattan Times that he and his wife Renee, the bakery's founder, set up shop in the 1980s when the neighborhood was considered dangerous, and that 3931 Broadway was their second shop. Patch reported that the other location, in Inwood, closed in early 2019 after its building lost gas service, and that the Inwood stretch of Broadway was co-named Renee Mancino Way in 2019.

## Honest verdict

A real neighborhood institution with a loyal following, and the carrot cake is the reason to go.
