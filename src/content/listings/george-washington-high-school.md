## What it is

The building at 549 Audubon Avenue, at W 193rd St in Fort George, opened in 1917 as an annex of Morris High School, according to Wikipedia. George Washington High School was founded in 1919 and moved into the building in 1925. Wikipedia says it stands on the site of the former Fort George Amusement Park. In 1999 the school was divided into four small high schools, and the building is now called the George Washington Educational Campus.

## Who it's for

History walkers who want to see where the neighborhood's best-known former residents went to school, and anyone following the history tour north from Fort Tryon and Bennett Park.

## Standout details

Wikipedia lists Henry Kissinger, the former Secretary of State and 1973 Nobel Peace Prize winner, among its alumni. His family reached Washington Heights in 1938, when it was a center of German-Jewish refugee life. The same list includes Alan Greenspan, the former Chairman of the Federal Reserve, the singer and actor Harry Belafonte, and the baseball player Manny Ramírez, who attended but did not graduate. Wikipedia flags parts of its alumni list as not fully verified; the four named here are widely reported.

## Honest verdict

There is nothing to tour: this is an active public school campus. Walk up to Audubon Avenue for the view of the building and the story, then keep going. Do not enter the grounds or photograph students.
