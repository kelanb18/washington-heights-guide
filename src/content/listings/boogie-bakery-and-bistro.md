## What it is

Boogie Bakery & Bistro is an all-day bakery and bistro at 4015 Broadway, at W 169th St, in the space many neighbors still call Coogan's. Delmonico's Hospitality Group opened it as the U.S. restaurant debut of Boogie Lab, a European sourdough brand that launched in Zagreb, Croatia. A Business Wire release dated October 15, 2026 says the full bakery and bistro opened that day; The Curious Uptowner reported that the bakery had already opened quietly in the weeks before, with the bistro still being finished.

## Who it's for

Hospital staff and students from the nearby Columbia medical campus, commuters on Broadway, and anyone who wants a loaf, a sandwich or a sit-down dinner in one place.

## Standout details

The company says every loaf, pastry and sandwich starts with flour, water, salt and a slow fermentation, and that bakers shape and bake in an open kitchen you can watch. The menu centers on breads, focaccia, sandwiches and sourdough pasta; The Curious Uptowner saw pastries such as a salted caramel pistachio croissant and guava donuts during the soft opening. Hours on the restaurant's website and in the press release are daily 6:45am to 7pm for the bakery and Monday to Saturday 5 to 10pm for the bistro.

## Honest verdict

A welcome new anchor for a corner with a lot of local history, but it is only weeks old, so menus, hours and quality may still settle. Coverage so far is opening-day news from Patch, The Curious Uptowner and the company's own release rather than reviews.
