## What it is

Manolo Tapas is a Spanish tapas restaurant and bar at 4165 Broadway, between W 176th and W 177th Sts and, its website notes, just across from the United Palace Theatre. The site's contact page invites reservations for lunch or dinner, takeout orders, catering, and private events in a private dining room it calls Salón Cova.

## Who it's for

Groups and couples looking for tapas and wine near the A train at 175th St, and people heading to a show at the United Palace. The site says the restaurant is a few blocks from the 175th St A station and has two parking garages between W 176th and W 177th on Broadway.

## Standout details

The website lists hours of 4pm to 10pm Monday through Sunday and a happy hour from 4pm to 6pm Monday to Friday. It also says the restaurant accepts cash only, with an ATM inside.

## Honest verdict

A handy tapas stop on the Broadway strip. The facts here come from the restaurant's own website, which may not have been updated recently; one aggregator lists Friday and Saturday hours until 11pm. Bring cash and call (212) 923-9100 to confirm before you plan around it.
