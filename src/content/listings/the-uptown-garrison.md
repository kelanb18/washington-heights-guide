## What it is

The Uptown Garrison is an all-day cafe, restaurant, and bar at 821 W 181st St, about a block from the 181st St A train. Its hours page lists separate services: coffee and pastries, counter-service lunch on weekdays, table-service brunch on weekends, dinner, and a bar with a daily happy hour from 4 to 6pm.

## Who it's for

Residents and visitors who want a place that can be a laptop morning, a weekend brunch, or an after-work drink. A 2023 Infatuation guide said it works for breakfast meetings, casual lunches, and a darker cocktail-bar mood in the evening.

## Standout details

The site lists the coffee and pastry service starting at 7:30am on weekdays and 9am on weekends. The 2023 Infatuation write-up mentioned pizzas and bar food, affordable cocktails, and wines on tap, and said it opened at 7am every day at that time; the current hours page shows later weekend openings, so go by the site.

## Honest verdict

Useful and adaptable, with different service styles by time of day: counter service at lunch, table service at brunch and dinner. That flexibility also means the experience changes depending on when you arrive. The details of the food and drink here come from a 2023 review and the cafe's own hours page, so confirm the menu before planning around a particular dish.
