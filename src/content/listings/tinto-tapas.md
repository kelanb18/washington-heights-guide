## What it is

Tinto Tapas Restaurant & Wine Bar is a Spanish tapas and wine bar at 209 Pinehurst Ave. Its own website says it was founded in 1998, so it has been around for more than two decades.

## Who it's for

Couples and small groups who want wine and shared plates on a residential street, and people who like wine tastings; the site promotes tasting events.

## Standout details

The restaurant's hours, per its site, are Tuesday to Thursday 4 to 10pm, Friday 4 to 11pm, Saturday noon to 11pm, and Sunday noon to 10pm, closed on Mondays. City inspection records show a visit on December 2, 2025.

## Honest verdict

A neighborhood mainstay rather than a new opening. I have not seen a recent independent review, so check the website for the current menu and events before booking, and note that the last inspection record I saw dates from December 2025.
