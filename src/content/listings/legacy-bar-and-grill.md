## What it is

Legacy Bar & Grill is a Latin American bar and grill at 4421 Broadway. Its own site describes Latin cuisine with a wine selection, meats and tapas, hand-crafted cocktails, and a rustic-chic bistro setting, and lists seating for 40 guests. It also does private parties, delivery, takeout and catering.

## Who it's for

People who want a drink and a plate near the north end of Washington Heights, a cocktail after dinner, or a small room for a group. It has a Sunday brunch.

## Standout details

According to the restaurant's website, hours are Monday to Thursday 4pm to midnight, Friday and Saturday 4pm to 2am, and Sunday 1pm to midnight. Happy hour is bar-only, Monday to Wednesday 4 to 8pm and Thursday to Saturday 4 to 7pm. Sunday brunch runs 1 to 6pm. Kitchen hours are shorter than bar hours. Restaurantji and Google-based listings show it open in October 2026, with a rating around 4.5 from about 187 Google reviews and happy hour on Monday to Wednesday.

## Honest verdict

A late-night option in a part of the neighborhood that has fewer of them, and it appears to be operating now. The phone number differs between sources: its website lists (917) 261-7785 and directories list (347) 931-7849, so try either before a late Friday or Saturday.
