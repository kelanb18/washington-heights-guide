## What it is

J. Hood Wright Park lies between W 173rd and W 176th Sts, from Fort Washington Ave to Haven Ave. NYC Parks says it is named for the former owner of the site, J. Hood Wright (1836–1894), a Philadelphia banker who lived in a mansion at W 175th St and Haven Ave and made large anonymous contributions to what is now the Washington Heights library branch. The city took the land in 1925 to create a park and playground, since the nearest playground was over a mile away.

## Who it's for

Families, dog owners, and ballplayers. NYC Parks lists basketball, handball, and volleyball courts, a multi-purpose field, a dog-walking area, a recreation center, spray showers, and a playground whose adventure equipment was custom-designed to resemble the George Washington Bridge.

## Standout details

The overlook at the northwest corner has views of the Hudson River and the George Washington Bridge. It also holds *3000 A.D. Diffusion Piece*, a 1974 sculpture by Terry Fugate-Wilcox made of magnesium and aluminum plates, which NYC Parks says is expected to diffuse by the year 3000. Rock formations along Haven Ave include a cave similar to the rock shelters in Inwood Hill Park.

## Honest verdict

A friendly, lived-in park that is a good stop for kids and for the view. NYC Parks says the recreation center has its own hours and programming, so check that page before planning around it.
