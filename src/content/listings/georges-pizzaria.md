## What it is

George's Pizzaria is a small counter-service pizza shop at 726 W 181st St, between Wadsworth and Fort Washington Aves. It has its own ordering website and a city inspection record, most recently on November 17, 2025.

## Who it's for

People who want a fast slice on the 181st St strip, takeout customers, and anyone who wants cheap, filling food.

## Standout details

The Infatuation's review says to order a slice, singles out the margherita for its mozzarella and dense, doughy crust, and says to get a side of garlic bread, which it describes as charred and crispy, painted with butter and topped with black pepper. The restaurant's own ordering page lists a menu with cheese pizza and a Greek salad.

## Honest verdict

A solid, uncomplicated slice shop. The ordering page shows 11am to 9:30pm for one day without listing the full week, so confirm hours before a late visit at (212) 568-6891.
