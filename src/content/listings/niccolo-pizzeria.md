## What it is

Niccolo Pizzeria is a small pizza and gelato shop at 2127 Amsterdam Ave, next door to Native Noodles. Its website describes a concept built around organic Italian flour, San Marzano tomatoes and olive oil sourced by owner Cleber, and lists pizza, focaccia, gelato, bomboloni and tiramisu.

## Who it's for

People who want a quick slice, a small sit-down lunch, or a soft-serve gelato in the Audubon area.

## Standout details

A May 2026 post by a visiting food blogger says the shop opened about a year and a half earlier, that its cheese slices had a very thin, crispy crust, and that Cleber explained the menu, offered gelato samples and recommended his house Calabrian chili oil. City inspection records show an inspection on June 15, 2026.

## Honest verdict

A friendly, small, and young business with a clear point of view. Independent coverage is limited to personal reviews, and hours disagree across listings, so I have omitted them; call (646) 964-5061 before you go.
