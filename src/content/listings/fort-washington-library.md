## What it is

The Fort Washington Library is a New York Public Library branch at 535 W 179th St. It is a Carnegie library designed by Cook & Welch, opened in April 1914, and built in the Italian Renaissance Revival style, according to Wikipedia. It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2020, and reopened after a renovation in early 2025.

## Who it's for

Neighbors who need books, computers or a quiet room, families, students, and architecture fans.

## Standout details

NYPL's own page says the branch's collections include Spanish and Russian materials, and it offers ESOL classes and a Teen Center. NYPL says notable library users have included Henry Kissinger, Lou Gehrig, Ralph Ellison, Maria Callas, Marianne Moore and Jacob Javits.

## Honest verdict

A civic landmark that is still used for what it was built for. Use NYPL's page for current hours, since I did not verify them directly.
