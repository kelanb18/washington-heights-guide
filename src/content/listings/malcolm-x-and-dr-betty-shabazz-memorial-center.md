## What it is

The Malcolm X & Dr. Betty Shabazz Memorial and Educational Center is in the restored Audubon Ballroom at 3940 Broadway, where Malcolm X was assassinated on February 21, 1965. The center's website says it opened in 2005 through the efforts of Dr. Betty Shabazz, the Shabazz family, Columbia University, and community advocates. Its permanent exhibition fills two floors and about 3,000 square feet.

## Who it's for

Visitors interested in Malcolm X, Dr. Shabazz, civil-rights history, and the Audubon Ballroom's own history. The center describes interactive touch-screen kiosks, videos, photographs, personal artifacts, and murals and a bronze statue in the ballroom.

## Standout details

The center's site lists adult admission at $20, seniors and children at $10, with advance purchase required, and hours Tuesday through Friday 10am to 6pm. The tours page does not list Saturday hours.

## Honest verdict

A meaningful place to learn the history of a specific site, and an important part of this neighborhood's story. Because tickets must be bought ahead and weekday hours are limited, plan before you go; ask the center directly about weekend visits.
