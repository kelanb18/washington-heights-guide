## What it is

The Fort Washington Greenmarket is a GrowNYC farmers market on Fort Washington Ave between W 168th and W 169th Sts. GrowNYC lists it as open on Tuesdays from 8am to 4pm during the 2026 season, which runs June 2 through December 15. Columbia University's community pages name Haven Plaza on Haven Ave as the access point. The market has run since 2010 and accepts SNAP and Health Bucks.

## Who it's for

Neighbors who cook, hospital workers on the Columbia campus, and anyone who wants regional produce, bread and other farm goods on a weekday.

## Standout details

It is one of the neighborhood's few weekday farmers markets, and the acceptance of SNAP and Health Bucks makes it more accessible than many. It is on the same block as the Fort Washington Avenue Armory.

## Honest verdict

A practical weekly market. Because it is seasonal, check GrowNYC's page before a late-fall visit, and note that vendors and hours can change.
