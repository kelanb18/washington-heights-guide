Wikipedia says filming also took place on 157th St and Riverside Drive in Washington Heights, and a New York Post photo gallery captions shots of Jolie scaling the wall of an apartment building as filming "in Washington Heights". This is the far southern edge of the neighborhood, outside the area covered by Hudson Heights. Most of the film was shot elsewhere in New York City.
