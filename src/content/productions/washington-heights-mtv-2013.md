The series ran January 9 to March 13, 2013 and followed nine primarily Dominican young adults who live in the neighborhood (Wikipedia). Goodwill NY/NJ's blog says MTV filmed a scene at its Washington Heights store at 512 W 181st St, with a still of the cast shopping there.
