Was In the Heights filmed in Washington Heights? Mostly, yes. Jon M. Chu shot the 2021 film of Lin-Manuel Miranda's musical on the streets it is set in, and Wikipedia confirms the bulk of filming was at W 175th St and Audubon Ave. Untapped New York's location guide traced the main scenes to the corners listed below, all within about a mile of each other.

The bodega exterior is the real STO Domingo Grocery at 196 Audubon Ave, redressed with a yellow awning and a mural, and the opening number was shot on that corner. J. Hood Wright Park hosts the Benny and Nina scenes, "Carnaval del Barrio" was shot at Haven Ave and W 176th St, "96,000" at the Highbridge Pool, and "Paciencia y Fe" in the graffiti-covered tunnel to the 191st St station.

Not everything was shot uptown. The bodega and salon interiors were sets built at the Armory in Brooklyn, the subway platform scenes were shot on an unused level of a Brooklyn station, and some scenes were filmed in Inwood, at Cooper Triangle in the East Village and at Astor Place. The United Palace, on Broadway at W 175th St, lists In the Heights among its production credits.
