Untapped New York's location guide places the bodega exterior (the real STO Domingo Grocery, redressed with a yellow awning and a mural) at 196 Audubon Ave and W 175th St, where the opening number was also shot. J. Hood Wright Park hosts the Benny and Nina scenes, with "Carnaval del Barrio" at Haven Ave and W 176th St. "96,000" was shot at the Highbridge Pool, and "Paciencia y Fe" in the graffiti-covered 191st St subway tunnel. Not everything was shot uptown: the bodega interior was a set built at the Armory in Brooklyn, the subway platform scenes were shot in Brooklyn, and some scenes were shot in Inwood and at Cooper Triangle. Wikipedia confirms the bulk of filming was at 175th St and Audubon Ave. The United Palace lists In the Heights among its production credits.
