Wikipedia's Highbridge Park article says the "Daisy Girl" ad was filmed in the park in the summer of 1964, citing USA Today, and its article on the ad describes three-year-old Monique Corzilius standing in a meadow in Highbridge Park counting petals. The ad aired during Lyndon Johnson's 1964 campaign against Barry Goldwater. Wikipedia is the source of the exact park name here; I did not open the USA Today piece.
