Patch reported that Madonna and Tokischa danced with a crowd on the corner of W 175th St and Audubon Ave on the night of Monday, September 5, 2022, in front of a corner bodega, and that police responded to noise complaints. Wikipedia says the video, directed by Sasha Kasiuha, was filmed that day in Washington Heights and shows a street party at that corner; Rhino confirms the video was released September 20, 2022. This is the same intersection used for the opening number of In the Heights.
