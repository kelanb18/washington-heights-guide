Wikipedia calls the lighthouse an important setting in the film's final scenes, and On the Set of New York lists the George Washington Bridge among its New York shooting locations. A reader comment on the Washington Heights NYC site describes John Garfield finding his brother's body on the rocks of Jeffrey's Hook. I could not confirm from a production source that the lighthouse scenes were shot on location rather than recreated.
