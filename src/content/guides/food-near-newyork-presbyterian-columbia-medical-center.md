This guide is for people spending a day, or a night, around NewYork-Presbyterian and Columbia's medical campus at W 168th St: patients and families, visitors, students, and staff between shifts. Everything here is a short walk from Fort Washington Ave and W 168th St, and the walking route starts from wherever you are.

The list runs from closest and quickest to a little farther afield, and ends with a florist and a park for when you need a break. Picks are editorial. Every place is a listing on Washington Heights Guide with its own page, sources and verdict. Walking times are estimates.

Hours change, and a few of these places do not post them online. Each listing page says what we could and could not confirm. This guide is independent and is not affiliated with the hospital or the university.
