The Cloisters sits at the top of Fort Tryon Park, and it is easy to head straight back downtown afterward. This guide is for walking instead: south through the park, past the Cabrini Shrine, and into Hudson Heights, where W 187th St has coffee, a bakery, a bookshop and dinner within a block.

The stops are in walking order, and the route button opens them in that order in Google Maps. The walk from the museum to W 187th St is roughly a mile on the park paths, mostly downhill. Picks are editorial. Every place is a listing on Washington Heights Guide with its own page, sources and verdict.

The museum closes on Wednesdays and Tinto closes on Mondays. Check each listing page for hours before you set out.
