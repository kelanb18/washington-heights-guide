## Radio Hotel

[Radio Hotel](https://www.theradiohotel.com/), 2420 Amsterdam Ave, New York, NY 10033, is a hotel in Washington Heights. The [Radio Restaurant](/places/radio-restaurant/) listing on this site places that address on Amsterdam Ave between W 181st and W 182nd Sts. The hotel site calls the property a Washington Heights hotel, with Radio Restaurant and an outdoor space, El Patio, in the building. Phone 929-688-4880. The FAQ says the front desk is staffed 24 hours, check-in is 3pm, and checkout is 11am.

## Hamilton House Inn

Nearby: Hamilton Heights, not in Washington Heights.

[The Hamilton House Inn](https://www.hamiltonhousenyc.com/), 327 Convent Avenue, New York, NY 10031, calls itself a boutique apartment-hotel in a 19th-century mansion on land that was part of Alexander Hamilton's estate, in Hamilton Heights. It says most units keep original details, including woodwork and fireplaces, and that check-in and checkout use codes. It says the inn is two blocks from the subway and does not name the station. Listed business hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm, and closed Saturday and Sunday. The site does not say whether those hours are the office or the building. Email book@hamiltonhousenyc.com. The site does not list a phone number, a room count, or a nightly rate.

Who it suits: families. The site says "Families welcome" and "Perfect for any family," and the stay is a short-term apartment with code entry. First-time visitors who want a 24-hour desk inside Washington Heights should start with Radio Hotel. Cloisters visitors start south of Fort Tryon Park. The inn is in Hamilton Heights, south of the medical campus at W 168th St, so families at the hospital should plan the trip. Confirm a weekend arrival, because entry is by code and the listed hours skip Saturday and Sunday.

## Aloft Harlem

Nearby: Harlem, not in Washington Heights.

[Aloft Harlem](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/nyclh-aloft-harlem/overview/), 2296 Frederick Douglass Boulevard, New York, NY 10027, phone 212-749-4000. Marriott's page places it in Harlem, near the Apollo Theater, Central Park, and Columbia University. That Columbia line sits with 125th Street landmarks, not the medical campus at W 168th St. Check-in is 3pm, checkout is noon, the minimum age to check in is 21, and the property is smoke-free. The page lists a 24-hour front desk, a fitness center, Re:fuel by Aloft for grab-and-go food, and the W XYZ bar. It says the hotel does not offer free breakfast. Transit listed on the page: the A, B, C, and D trains, the 2 and 3 trains, and Metro-North at Harlem–125th Street.

Pets are welcome, up to two per room, 40 pounds each, with a non-refundable fee of $75 per pet per stay (checked Nov 11, 2026 — check current price). Valet parking is $55 plus tax per day, sedans only, with no in-and-out privileges (checked Nov 11, 2026 — check current price). The page lists complimentary Wi-Fi and also says Wi-Fi is free for Marriott Bonvoy members, so ask whether a fee applies if you are not a member.

Who it suits: first-time visitors who want a staffed hotel, a bar, and the subway at 125th Street, and who are staying in Harlem on purpose. Cloisters visitors are south of Fort Tryon Park. The hotel lists the A train, and it does not say that train serves the museum. The hotel is in Harlem, south of the medical campus at W 168th St, and the minimum check-in age is 21. This is a Harlem hotel.

The overview places it on West 125th Street near the Apollo Theater, Columbia University, and Central Park, and says guestrooms offer views of Central Park, the Hudson River, and the skyline. Read "Columbia" there as the campus the overview pairs with 125th Street, not Columbia University Irving Medical Center. Marriott Bonvoy members who book direct get complimentary in-room Wi-Fi. Off-site parking is listed at Harlem Renaissance Parking, 232 W 126th St, 0.2 miles away. The page does not list a parking price.

Who it suits: first-time visitors who want a full hotel and a restaurant on 125th Street, and people visiting the Columbia campus the hotel pairs with that street. The page says pets are not allowed. A family at the medical center would be staying in Harlem, south of W 168th St, with a 4pm check-in and a minimum check-in age of 21. Cloisters visitors are south of Fort Tryon Park. This is a Harlem hotel.
