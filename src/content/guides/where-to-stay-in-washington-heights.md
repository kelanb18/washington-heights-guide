Washington Heights has very few hotels, and most visitors to the neighborhood stay farther downtown. This guide starts with Radio Hotel on Amsterdam Ave at W 181st St, then lists two hotels in Harlem, about 55 blocks south, that connect to the neighborhood on the A train.

Details come from each hotel's own website unless the entry names another source. Room rates are not listed here because they change daily. Fees and policies change too, so confirm them with the hotel before you book. Radio Restaurant, the hotel's ground-floor restaurant and bar, has its own [listing](/places/radio-restaurant).
