United Palace, the landmarked 1930 theater at 4140 Broadway, hosts concerts, film screenings, tours and community events. This guide is for the hour before and the hour after: a place to eat within a few minutes' walk, and somewhere still open when the show lets out.

Everything here is within about five minutes of the theater at Broadway and W 175th St. Picks are editorial. Every place is a listing on Washington Heights Guide with its own page, sources and verdict.

Check the United Palace calendar for door times before you plan dinner, and confirm restaurant hours on the listing pages.
