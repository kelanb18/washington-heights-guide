This is a landmark-heavy stretch of the Broadway valley in Washington Heights. The United Palace, a 1930 theater at 4140 Broadway built as Loew's 175th Street Theatre, anchors the block, and Malecón, the Dominican restaurant at 4141 Broadway, is directly across the street. Next to the theater, W 175th St between Broadway and Wadsworth Ave was turned into Plaza de las Americas, a pedestrian plaza that the city opened in 2015.

According to the city's 2015 announcement, the plaza added benches, lighting, trees, and a public water-fountain artwork by Ester Partegàs, and was designed to support the vendor market that had used that block for years and the GrowNYC Greenmarket held there. The city said it can also host public events such as craft fairs and performances.

The 175th St A station is here, and the MTA's station list marks it as accessible, along with 168th St (A and C) and 181st St (A). Wikipedia notes that the connecting tunnel to the George Washington Bridge Bus Station is not accessible. J. Hood Wright Park is at the station entrance.
