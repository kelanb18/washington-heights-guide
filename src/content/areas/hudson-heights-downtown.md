"Downtown" and "uptown" are how locals sort Hudson Heights' two small commercial strips; neither term is official. The downtown strip is W 181st St where it meets Fort Washington Ave, a short walk from the 181st St A station and its elevators to the ridge. The Washington Heights Business Improvement District's W 181st St corridor runs from Amsterdam Ave west to Fort Washington Ave, so this is where that corridor ends.

Addresses on this stretch are in the 700s and 800s on W 181st St. Two places confirmed for this guide are The Uptown Garrison (821 W 181st St), an all-day coffee, brunch, and cocktail room, and Le Chéile (839 W 181st St), an Irish pub. A 2023 Infatuation guide also listed a pizza counter, a sushi restaurant, and an Italian restaurant on the same blocks; those have not been re-checked here.

The George Washington Bridge and its bus terminal, between W 178th and 179th Sts, are close by, which is part of why the strip is busy with commuters as well as residents. It is a short walk from here to the Hudson River Greenway and the Little Red Lighthouse below the bridge, and to Fort Tryon Park to the north.
