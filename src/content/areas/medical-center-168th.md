The hospital campus sets the rhythm here: scrubs on the sidewalk, ambulances on Fort Washington Ave and a lunch rush on Broadway. The 168th St station is served by the A, C and 1 trains.

Fort Washington Ave between W 168th and W 169th Sts has the Fort Washington Avenue Armory, now a track and field center, and a seasonal Tuesday greenmarket. Broadway between W 168th and W 170th Sts is lined with bakeries, delis and restaurants that feed the hospital crowd. West of the campus, Haven Ave and Riverside Dr are quieter residential streets above the river.
