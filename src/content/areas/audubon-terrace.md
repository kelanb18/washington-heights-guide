This is where Washington Heights begins at its southern edge. Audubon Terrace takes up the block of Broadway between W 155th and W 156th Sts. Wikipedia says it was built between 1904 and 1930 under Archer Huntington, opened with the Hispanic Society of America in 1907, and still holds the Hispanic Society, the American Academy of Arts and Letters and Our Lady of Esperanza Church. It is a city landmark and sits on land that was once part of John James Audubon's estate, which is where the old Audubon Park name comes from.

Across W 155th St is Trinity Church Cemetery. North of the Terrace, Broadway is a working stretch of bars, cafes and small shops, and the side streets run downhill to Riverside Drive and the river.
