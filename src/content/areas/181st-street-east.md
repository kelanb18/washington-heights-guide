The Washington Heights Business Improvement District, founded in 1983, describes its district as 245 commercial storefronts across 29 blocks: W 181st St from Amsterdam Ave to Fort Washington Ave, Broadway between W 179th and 182nd Sts, and St. Nicholas Ave between W 179th and 183rd Sts.

The corner of W 181st St and Broadway has a long history. The Blue Bell Tavern stood on the northwest corner in the 18th century, and in 1915 the 3,500-seat Coliseum Theatre was built on the site. It was demolished in 2021 after the city denied it landmark status, according to Wikipedia. Several Bronx bus routes (Bx3, Bx11, Bx13, Bx35, Bx36) run along W 181st St.

It works as both a shopping street and a transit corridor: the 1 and A trains each stop at 181st St, and the street is a crosstown route for several buses.
