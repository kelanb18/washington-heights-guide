This is the Washington Heights most people who live here would recognize first: tall prewar apartment buildings, Spanish on every corner, and avenues lined with bakeries, corner stores and small restaurants. Wikipedia's Washington Heights article calls the neighborhood home to the most prominent Dominican community in the United States, and these blocks are a big part of that.

St. Nicholas Ave and Audubon Ave run north through the area, with W 175th St crossing at the United Palace end. The Fort Washington branch of the library is on W 179th St, Highbridge Park and its pool are at the eastern edge, and the 175th St and 181st St stations are a short walk away.
