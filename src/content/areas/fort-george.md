Fort George is the lesser-known sibling of Hudson Heights on the other side of Broadway. It is named for the Revolutionary War's Fort George, according to Wikipedia, and runs east of Broadway from W 181st St to Dyckman St, where Washington Heights meets Inwood. Dyckman St is the neighborhood's northern edge, so it is not treated as a separate area here.

Notable places include Yeshiva University's Wilf Campus (between W 184th and 186th Sts, east of Broadway), George Washington High School at W 192nd to 193rd Sts and Audubon Ave, built on the site of the original Fort George, and Washington Terrace, a rare semi-private street that runs south from W 186th St for a half-block between Audubon and Amsterdam Aves.

Highbridge Park's northern section, Fort George Hill, was formed from the former Fort George Amusement Park, a trolley park built in 1895. The Parks Department opened a network of mountain-bike trails there in 2007, per Wikipedia. The park runs along the Harlem River side of Washington Heights from W 155th St to Dyckman St.
