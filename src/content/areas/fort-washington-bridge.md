The George Washington Bridge shapes this part of the Heights. Its approaches cut across the blocks around W 178th and W 179th Sts, and the George Washington Bridge Bus Station sits above them between Fort Washington and Wadsworth Aves. Wikipedia says the station opened in 1963 and was designed by the Italian engineer Pier Luigi Nervi, and it still serves commuter buses to New Jersey.

South of the bridge, J. Hood Wright Park sits on a hilltop between Haven Ave and Fort Washington Ave, with views of the bridge. Below it, Fort Washington Park runs along the river to the Little Red Lighthouse at Jeffrey's Hook. Some people count the blocks north of W 177th St as Hudson Heights. We keep them separate because the bridge, not the ridge, defines them.
