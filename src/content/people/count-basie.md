Count Basie is listed among the notable residents of 555 Edgecombe Avenue, the 1914–16 apartment building at W 160th St. Wikipedia says the corner outside was co-named Paul Robeson Boulevard and Count Basie Place in October 2009 after advocacy by the Harlem Historical Society and the Morris-Jumel Mansion's director. The exact years he lived in the building were not confirmed in the sources used.
