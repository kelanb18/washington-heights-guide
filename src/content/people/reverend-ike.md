Wikipedia's Washington Heights article says the United Palace was built in 1930 as the Loew's 175th Street Theater, designed primarily by Thomas W. Lamb with interior work by Harold Rambusch. In 1969, the televangelist Reverend Ike bought it, and it became a church for his United Church Science of Living Institute.

New York City made the building a landmark in 2016. Today the United Palace also operates as a cultural center, with concerts, films, and live performances. The building is at 4140 Broadway, across from Malecón, near Plaza de las Americas.
