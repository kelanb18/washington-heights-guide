Lin-Manuel Miranda lives uptown, in Hudson Heights.

He has been uptown most of his life, though not always in the Heights. The New York Times reported in 2008 that he grew up in Inwood, just north, and that as a kid he took piano lessons on W 181st Street. He set In the Heights here because, as he put it, Washington Heights "sings better."

His other documented ties to the neighborhood: in 2014, the Morris-Jumel Mansion says, he sat in Aaron Burr's bedchamber and wrote songs for Hamilton there, which he describes in a PBS clip as being "artist-in-residence literally." In 2018, NBC New York reported that he and his family helped broker a deal to keep Coogan's, the Broadway pub at 169th Street, open after it announced it would close over rising rent. Coogan's closed for good in April 2020.
